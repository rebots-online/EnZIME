A few useful distinctions.

Can I read Catholic works from Sanctissimissa?

The shared reader is designed for its bookstore, including original PDF pages, long-form text, and media. The storefront adapter and edition-level rights catalogue remain integration work.

What works in this preview?

Local import, PDF pages and zoom, text and media reading, saved place and notes, immutable final text, and the preserved Pysanky demo. Full ZIM rendering, local AI, EPUB and live entitlement activation are release gates.

Are payments live?

A configured checkout opens RevenueCat’s hosted paywall. This preview does not issue paid entitlements. Activation requires the verified BIDLR provider integration and a complete purchase/restore/refund test.
