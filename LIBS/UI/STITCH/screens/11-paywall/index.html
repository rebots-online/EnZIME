The Architecture of Local-First Systems

PUBLISHED: OCT 24, 2023 · 12 MIN READ

The shift towards cloud-based software has provided unprecedented convenience, yet it has come at a cost: the loss of agency over our own data. EnZIME is built on the premise that your digital workspace should be resilient to network failures and corporate shifts.

Local-first software combines the best of both worlds: the collaboration capabilities of the cloud with the performance and reliability of local software. By prioritizing local storage and using synchronization as a secondary mechanism, we ensure that the user is always in control.
